Texas drilling permits, September 2026: 682 new wells, most in Martin County

New-well permits rose 15% on August, and 86% of them are horizontal. Here are the counties and the operators.

Royalty Clerk · Oct 31, 2026

The Railroad Commission issued 761 drilling permits in September 2026. 682 were for new wells, up 14.8% on August (594). 590 of the new wells, or 86.5%, are horizontal. The other permits were for recompletions (63), re-entries (11) and smaller changes.

New-well permits have stayed in a narrow band all year: 660 in April, 652 in May, 684 in June, 611 in July, 594 in August and 682 in September. October stood at 656 as of October 30.

What a permit near you means

A permit is the first public sign of a new well. Drilling, completion and first sales come later, often many months later, and not every permit gets drilled. If a new well does produce from your minerals, expect a division order from the operator. Under Texas law, the first payment is due within 120 days after the end of the month of first sale.

Texas well watch lists new permits county by county, so you can see what is planned near your land.
